Which agreement are we auditing?

Start with the one that costs the most every month. Copiers, phones, software, merchant services, waste, security — any recurring vendor works.

1

The quote is not the contract

The proposal is a sales document. The signed agreement is the only one a court or a collections department will ever read. Anything promised in one and missing from the other doesn't exist.

Put the proposal next to the signed agreement. For each promise you were given, mark whether it appears in the signed agreement — not the proposal, not an email.

Promise you were givenIn the signed agreement?
Fix: every missing promise goes into a signed addendum, or it doesn't exist.
2

One payment, two contracts

The equipment is usually a lease with a finance company. The service is with the dealer. One invoice, two agreements, two end dates — and only one of them is negotiable after you sign.

List every agreement tied to this one payment. If the end dates don't match, you'll be paying for one after the other is gone.

Fix: write every end date on one page. More than one date means more than one contract.
3

The minimum is your real price

Minimum volume, minimum charge, minimum spend — whatever it's called, it's the floor you pay whether you use it or not. Get smaller, and your real per-unit cost climbs.

Fix: pull twelve months of usage. Under the minimum most months? You're renting a number.
4

The rate you signed is a starting rate

Look for "adjustment", "escalation" or "annual increase". It often reads like a ceiling — "up to 10% annually". In practice, the ceiling is the plan.

Fix: compute the final-year payment before you sign — and get the cap in writing.
5

Leaving costs money

Every agreement has an end, and the end has a price that was never in the proposal. You find out what it costs at the moment you have the least leverage. Find out now instead.

What leaving will cost — put a number on each

Ask the vendor for these in writing now, well before the end of term, while nobody needs anything from you.

Fix: put the notice deadline in your calendar today and get the exit costs in writing.
✉

The non-renewal letter

If the agreement renews on its own, a short written notice inside the window stops it. Sending it doesn't mean you're leaving — it means the renewal becomes a negotiation instead of a default.

Check the agreement's notices clause for the required method — many specify certified mail or a specific address. Send it so you can prove delivery and the date. This is a template, not legal advice.

I've Read Over 1,000 Vendor Contracts. They All Hide the Same 5 Things.

After a while you stop reading the contract and start reading the pattern.

What this episode covers

The same five things show up in vendor agreements across every industry — equipment, software, telecom, waste, payment processing. None of them are scams. All of them are defensible. And every one of them moves risk from the vendor onto you at the moment you’re least likely to notice. Here’s where each one lives in the document, and how to check yours this week.
